About the Project

Disability Representation in Children’s Books is a project led by Dr. Carly Christensen, partially funded by Edith Lando Virtual Learning Centre and developed as part of the work of Canadian Institute for Inclusion and Citizenship (CIIC).

The project supports inclusive education by enhancing critical literacy around disability in children’s literature and curating resources that critically engage with disability representation.

Grounded in disability justice, the project moves beyond ableist and deficit-based narratives to celebrate disabled identities, experiences, and communities while supporting educators in creating more accessible, inclusive, and affirming classrooms.

Advisories on Accessibility & Representation

This project is grounded in disability justice, neurodiversity-affirming practice, and the social model of disability. We recognize disability as shaped not only by individual experiences or differences, but also by social, cultural, physical, and environmental contexts.

Disability categories in this project are used as broad organizational tools for accessibility, searching, and educational use. They are not intended to reflect fixed, deficit-based, or exhaustive understandings of disability.

In this project, “ND” is used to refer to both neurodiversity and neurodivergent experiences and identities.

Race and ethnicity categories are also used as general organizational tools to support accessibility and navigation. They are not intended to fully represent the complexity or diversity of racial and ethnic identities. Where specific racial or ethnic identities are identified, these descriptions are based on how identities are explicitly presented in the book rather than labels assigned by our team.

Website Accessibility Features

We have designed this website with a range of user preferences in mind. The accessibility menu allows users to customize the visual presentation of the site, including:

  • Colour theme: Light, Dark, and High Contrast
  • Saturation: Normal, Low, Grayscale, and Highlight Links
  • Images and motion: Hide Images and Reduce Motion
  • Text size: Small through Very Large
  • Font: Default, Lexend, and OpenDyslexic
  • Line height: Normal, Relaxed, and Extra Large
  • Text spacing: Normal, Wide, and Extra Wide

Users can adjust these settings at any time using the accessibility controls on the website and can clear their preferences when desired.

Images and Alternative Text

Book cover images are included primarily for visual and decorative purposes. Essential information including the book title, author, disability categories, and other relevant descriptors, is provided as text and is available to screen readers and other assistive technologies.

We have not added separate alt text to book cover images because the essential information conveyed by the images is already provided in the surrounding text. This approach is intended to avoid redundant information for screen reader users while keeping the browsing experience streamlined.
